# TMO Piano — credits and licences

This folder hosts the sampled grand piano used by The Maestro Online's widgets.

## Piano samples
**Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5), recorded and released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 licence (CC BY 3.0): http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Original: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
The files here are the MP3/OGG conversions prepared by Yotam Mann for Tone Piano
(https://github.com/tambien/Piano). The recordings are unchanged.
